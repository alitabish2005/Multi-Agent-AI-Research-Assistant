# NexusAI

Autonomous multi-agent research engine for deep technical and scientific exploration across arXiv, academic literature, and live web sources.

### Capabilities
- **Collaborative Reasoning:** Specialized agents coordinate to scope, plan, investigate, verify, and synthesize complex technical questions.
- **Autonomous Multi-Source Research:** Concurrently queries arXiv preprints, academic publications, and web sources.
- **Live Evidence Verification:** Evaluates source credibility and synthesizes findings into publication-grade reports.
